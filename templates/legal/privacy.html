Privacy notice

Template notice — have counsel review before commercial use.

What we hold

  • Account details: username, email, name.
  • Organization membership, course and cohort enrolment.
  • Assessment records: attempts, answers, save history, scores, timing.
  • Approved timing accommodations, including the reason recorded by your institution.
  • Technical records attached to answer saves: IP address and browser user-agent, kept as assessment-integrity evidence.

Who controls it

Your institution is the data controller. QuizOps processes this data on their instructions. Requests about grades, accommodations or enrolment go to your institution.

Your data, exported

Your account page exports everything listed above as JSON, at any time.

Deletion, and what survives it

Deleting your account removes your name, email, login and the IP and user-agent records attached to your saves. Your assessment results, finalization records and audit entries are retained as anonymous records linked only to an opaque identifier.

We do this because grading history is an institutional record that schools are normally required to keep, and because an audit trail that can be erased on request is not an audit trail. If you need the underlying results removed as well, that is your institution's decision to make.

Retention

  • Assessment records: retained per your institution's policy.
  • Answer-save events including IP/user-agent: 24 months, then the network identifiers are cleared and the revision history is kept.
  • Notifications: cleared when the account is deleted.

Sub-processors and location

QuizOps runs on infrastructure operated by your institution or its hosting provider. It makes no calls to third-party services, sends data to no analytics provider, and includes no third-party trackers. Transactional email is delivered through the SMTP provider your institution configures.

Security

  • Passwords are stored using Django's PBKDF2 hasher with a per-user salt; we never see your password.
  • Traffic is served over HTTPS with HSTS in production.
  • Answer keys are never sent to the browser during an in-progress attempt.
  • Data is isolated per organization and access is checked on the server for every request.
